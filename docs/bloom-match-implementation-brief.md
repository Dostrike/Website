## Initial web scope and rules

Use an8×8 full garden with six flower kinds, own code-native silhouettes/patterns and distinct readable names. Initial web scope uses full beds and collect-kind goals; holes/weeds are deferred and must not appear as advertised features. Offer18-,24- and30-move gardens, plus a date-seeded30-move daily. Do not label this as an imported app level30 or an established difficulty/strength calibration.

Ordinary swaps are orthogonal adjacent exchanges that create a horizontal/vertical run of at least three involving an exchanged cell. Same-kind ordinary no-op and diagonal/nonmatching swaps consume no move and no random draw. Stable starting/restored boards contain no unresolved match and have a legal swap. Special combinations are explicitly allowed exceptions to the ordinary match requirement.

Find maximal runs simultaneously and merge overlapping matched runs into connected components. Choose creation priority: length-five-or-more line creates a rainbow; intersecting horizontal/vertical runs create a ring; otherwise length-four creates a stripe oriented along its matching line. Create at most one special per component. Prefer the swap destination if in the component, then source, then lowest row-major matched cell. Cascades use the lowest row-major cell. These priorities and anchors must be executable fixtures before generator/UI work.

The newly created special remains at its anchor and is protected for its creation wave; it is not instantly detonated. Existing specials reached by that wave activate through a deduplicated queue. A stripe clears its whole row/column; a ring clears a clipped3×3 region. A rainbow exchanged with a non-rainbow clears its partner kind plus itself and triggers reached existing specials. Two rainbows clear the whole bed. Other special pairs activate both ordinary footprints at their post-swap locations, with recursive reached-special activation and set-union clearing. Rainbow plus another special also triggers the partner's footprint; it does not convert every matching flower into a copied special mechanic. State these chosen web rules in the guide.

Count each actually removed flower once per wave toward its kind goal and score. A bloom converted into a protected new special is not removed that wave and earns no collection/clear points. Score10 per removed bloom times the cascade-wave multiplier, capped at5. A new player/helper action restarts the multiplier at1. Pack columns downward, refill from a versioned seeded stream, and resolve complete waves until stable before checking goals or exhausted moves. Meeting goals on the final legal move wins. Exact snapshots/replay must include random-stream position so Undo/reload cannot reroll a cascade.
